The 1031, DST and 721 UPREIT Path: Code Sections at Each Step

Published October 17, 2026 · 721 UPREIT Pros

This note maps the tax rules that apply at each step of a common deferral path: a Section 1031 exchange into a Delaware Statutory Trust, a holding period, and a Section 721 contribution of the trust's property to a REIT operating partnership.

Step 1: Section 1031 into a DST

  • Section 1031(a)(3): replacement property must be identified within 45 days and received within 180 days of the transfer.
  • Rev. Rul. 2004-86: a properly structured DST interest is treated as a direct interest in real property, so it can be like-kind replacement property.
  • Basis carries over under Section 1031(d), adjusted for boot.

Process detail is covered in this guide to a 1031 exchange into a Delaware Statutory Trust.

Step 2: The DST holding period

  • The trust's limited powers under Rev. Rul. 2004-86 restrict new capital, refinancing and lease renegotiation.
  • Exit timing is set by the sponsor. Some offerings include a fair market value option for an affiliated operating partnership to acquire the property.

See DST holding period and exit timing considerations.

Step 3: Section 721 contribution

  • Section 721(a): no gain or loss on contributing property to a partnership for an interest.
  • Section 722: outside basis in OP units equals the contributed basis, adjusted for liabilities under Section 752.
  • Section 704(c): built-in gain is allocated back to the contributor if the partnership sells the property.
  • Section 707: cash or debt relief within about two years may be a disguised sale.

OP units are partnership interests, which Section 1031(a)(2) excludes, so later 1031 exchanges are generally unavailable. The two-step DST to 721 UPREIT strategy and this DST-to-721 document review checklist cover the practical side.
